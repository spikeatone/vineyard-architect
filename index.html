Frequently asked

What's in the free version?

The whole game. Every feature is unlocked — the free tier caps the size of your estate (2 vineyard sites) rather than locking features away. Vineyard Architect Pro removes the cap and lets you acquire existing wineries.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the Go Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you're signed in with the same one you purchased on.

Why isn't my vineyard making wine yet?

Newly planted vines take about three years to bear their first commercial crop, and a few more to reach full quality — that patience is the heart of the game. Use the speed controls on the Grow and Ops tabs to move through the quiet years, and slow down for harvest and weather.

How do I sell my wine?

Each vintage ages in your cellar and then releases. Sell released lots on the Ops tab, splitting them across your tasting room, wine club, and distributors — each pays a different price and has its own limits.

Can I continue the same game on my iPhone and iPad?

Saves live on the device you play on, in up to three slots. They don't sync between devices yet, so your iPhone and iPad each keep their own estates. Save before you switch devices.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; Pro stays active until the current period ends.

I lost a saved game.

Saves live on your device, and the game keeps up to three slots — if one looks empty, check the others. We can't recover a deleted save remotely, since the game never sends us your data.
